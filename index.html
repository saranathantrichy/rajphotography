About Us

Crafting Stories
Frame by Frame

At RAJ PHOTOGRAPHY, every wedding is a unique narrative waiting to be told. Based in Ariyalur, we blend cinematic vision with heartfelt storytelling — capturing the emotions, rituals, and candid moments that define your celebration.

From traditional Tamil ceremonies filled with vibrant colours and sacred rituals, to intimate couple portraits bathed in golden light — our lens finds the extraordinary in every ordinary moment.

With premium drone coverage, luxury album design, and cinematic video edits, we deliver a complete visual legacy of your most cherished day.
